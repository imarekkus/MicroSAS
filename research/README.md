## Evidence standards

Separate evidence into:
1. **Verified:** primary-source pricing, product functionality, documented policies, observed transactions or directly interviewed buyers.
2. **Reported:** founder disclosures, public revenue dashboards, vendor case studies and review claims. Note provenance and date.
3. **Hypothesis:** untested product gap, willingness to pay, customer acquisition, conversion, margin or demand forecast.

For each opportunity document: customer, exact recurring job, evidence of pain, existing alternatives, specific gap, distribution route, MVP scope, pricing hypothesis, risks, disconfirming evidence, and validation experiment.

Do **not** infer that a business is vulnerable merely because a competitor reports high MRR. Do not present founder-reported revenue as audited profit. Record research dates, links, and contrary evidence.

Be skeptical of developer-led market research threads, crossposts, promotional testimonials and competitor-authored material; these do not independently establish customer demand.
