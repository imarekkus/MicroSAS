# Roadmap

> Planning document, not a commitment to launch dates.

## Phase 0 — Repository setup
- [x] Establish mission, research principles and starter templates
- [x] Create the remote private repository and push initial files

## Phase 1 — Opportunity discovery (not started)
- [ ] Collect a broad pool of concrete problems from searchable customer evidence
- [ ] Document incumbents, pricing, customer pain and likely buyers
- [ ] Identify a credible acquisition channel for each serious candidate
- [ ] Shortlist and rank opportunities with evidence quality clearly marked

**Gate:** select 2–3 problems with observable pain, signs of spending, reachable buyers, and manageable scope.

## Phase 2 — Validation
- [ ] Conduct non-leading interviews / direct customer outreach
- [ ] Test willingness to pay (pre-sale, paid pilot, or purchase intent with a concrete price)
- [ ] Estimate first-customer acquisition costs and operating economics

**Gate:** demonstrate real buyer commitment before building extensively.

## Phase 3 — MVP and launch
- [ ] Choose stack based on the actual problem
- [ ] Ship the smallest reliable paid workflow
- [ ] Instrument activation, conversion, retention and support requests

## Phase 4 — Operate, scale or pivot
- [ ] Reach the first paying customer
- [ ] Work toward $1,000 MRR, then $3,000 MRR
- [ ] Review churn, customer acquisition, margins and founder time
- [ ] Scale, reposition, or pivot based on data
