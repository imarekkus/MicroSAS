# MicroSAS

A lean, evidence-led project for discovering, validating, launching, and potentially scaling small software businesses.

## Goal

Build a solo-developer-friendly app or SaaS capable of reaching **$1,000–$3,000 MRR**. If early retention and acquisition economics justify it, scale beyond that. Otherwise, learn and pivot with minimal sunk cost.

These are **targets**, not revenue forecasts.

## Principles

- **Demand before code:** start from costly, recurring problems and evidence that people pay to solve them.
- **Distribution from day one:** identify a realistic path to the first 10 paying customers without relying on an existing audience.
- **Smallest useful product:** favor narrowly scoped MVPs over complete platforms.
- **Solo-founder economics:** track time, recurring costs, customer acquisition, margin, retention, and support burden.
- **No forced commitment:** reject or pivot when evidence fails; preserve reusable learnings.
- **Explore broadly:** niches are not limited to existing expertise (e.g., extensions, marketplaces, B2B tools, creator utilities).

## Success measures

**Initial:** first customer, first $100 MRR, then $1k MRR. **Validation:** $3k MRR with acceptable retention, support load, and acquisition economics. **Scale/pivot:** expand only if validated economics and demand support it.
