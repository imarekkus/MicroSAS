# Problem Discovery Interview

Goal: understand actual behavior and costs, not collect compliments for a pitch.

1. Tell me about the last time you encountered **[specific problem]**. What triggered it?
2. Walk me through what you did, step by step.
3. How frequently has it happened during the past 3 months?
4. What happened when it went wrong? What did it cost in time, money, or missed work?
5. Which tools, workarounds or people did you use? What do those cost today?
6. Have you tried to fix or replace that process? What happened?
7. Who decides whether to pay for tooling like this?
8. If possible, can you show me a redacted recent example?
